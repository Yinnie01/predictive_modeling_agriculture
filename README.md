### Project FarmWise: Intelligent Decision Support System for Data-Driven Crop Selection and Management

### Project Description and Objectives

In this project, I created an advanced decision-support system aimed at assisting farmers in selecting the most suitable crop varieties based on essential soil metrics, including nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P), potassium (K), and pH levels. By leveraging a supervised machine learning approach, specifically the Decision Tree Model, I predicted optimal crops for specific soil nutrient profiles. 

This initiative addressed the challenges farmers faced due to the high costs and time associated with comprehensive soil testing, which often led to suboptimal crop choices driven by financial limitations. Utilizing the training dataset which captured various soil metrics alongside crop suitability, I developed multi-class classification models. These models enhanced predictive accuracy regarding which crops would thrive under specific soil conditions, ultimately optimizing growth potential and maximizing agricultural yields.

### Methodology

- **Soil Condition Assessment:** I systematically quantified essential soil metrics (N, P, K, and pH) to comprehensively evaluate soil quality and its compatibility with different crop types.

- **Development of Crop Prediction Models:** My approach incorporated advanced machine learning techniques, including decision trees, random forests, and support vector machines. I analyzed historical dataset patterns to identify the best-suited crops for given soil conditions.

- **Feature Importance Analysis:** I conducted an in-depth analysis to identify the most influential soil metric affecting crop selection. This analysis helped farmers focus on the key measurements necessary for making informed decisions about crop planting.

- **Support for Sustainable Agricultural Practices:** The project culminated in the development of a decision-support framework that provided actionable insights to farmers. This empowered them to improve crop yields while adhering to budget constraints and promoting soil health sustainability, integrating best practices derived from my analysis to encourage environmentally responsible farming decisions.

### Conclusion

The analysis of available data indicated that potassium (K) is the most significant predictive feature, as it demonstrates the highest f1_score. This finding suggested that farmers can prioritize testing for potassium levels in their soil to accurately determine the most appropriate crop types to plant, thereby optimizing their resources and enhancing agricultural productivity.
